# Sample knowledge base

## Kyoto Tips
- Best time to visit temples is early morning (before 8 AM) to avoid crowds.
- Many restaurants close between 3-5 PM. Plan meals accordingly.
- JR Pass is useful if you plan day trips to Nara or Osaka.
- Autumn (November) has beautiful foliage but higher hotel prices.

## General Travel Advice
- Always carry a portable charger and a universal adapter.
- Download offline maps before you leave.
- Keep a digital copy of your passport.
- Budget an extra 10-15% for unexpected expenses.
